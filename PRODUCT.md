# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People using ReplyAI to draft and manage AI-assisted replies. Authentication should get them into their workspace quickly with minimal distraction.

## Product Purpose

ReplyAI helps users produce useful replies faster. The authentication surface exists to establish trust, clearly explain access, and route users into the protected product.

## Brand Personality

Focused, capable, and restrained. Copy is concise and practical.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic purple SaaS styling, decorative gradients, glass effects, oversized marketing copy, and novelty interactions in core workflows.

## Design Principles

- Keep the primary task obvious.
- Use familiar controls and predictable interaction patterns.
- Let typography and spacing establish hierarchy.
- Reserve accent color for actions and state.
- Make authentication responsive and accessible by default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, clear error states, reduced-motion compatibility, and touch targets suitable for mobile use.
